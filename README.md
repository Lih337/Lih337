### 🪐 PlanetVerse (VR for Education)
Team V-01 project: explore **all eight planets** of the Solar System in VR and compare terrain, atmosphere and gravity.
- **Problem:** students struggle to picture planetary science from 2D textbooks
- **Solution:** a Solar System hub where learners travel to each planet, read or listen to descriptions, and take quizzes
- **My role:** researched the scientific information, wrote the descriptions, and created the quiz used to test users
- **Result (from our final report):** the prototype was demonstrated on demo day, and students reported higher engagement and curiosity than with textbook lessons

### 🚢 TitanLift (C + GTK)
Team project for Algorithm & Computational Thinking 2.
- Priority queue: **ALARM > VIP > NORMAL**; VIP floor 9 only with a valid card scan
- Capacity limit of 8 passengers / 560 kg, emergency alarm mode and a live activity log
- **My role:** Titanic-themed research and storytelling, all slides, flowcharts and the written report

### 🐵 Monkey Banana Adventure (Scratch)
Individual project for Algorithm & Computational Thinking. **I designed, coded and documented it myself.**
- A monkey catches falling bananas; jar, ball and milk give no score
- Models a simple ecosystem: banana = producer, monkey = consumer
- Levels switch at 25, 50 and 75 points, and the score resets each level
- Includes flowcharts for every character and the full Scratch code in the report

### 💬 Unicom (Figma)
